# Independent project review — 2026-10-17

Scope: repository `bariscelikk1/cALorie`, branch `feat/tcn-rebuild`, commit `85796f7`; code, saved model reports, manifests, split assignments, and the current external creator review. This is an evidence audit, not a new model run or a new label decision.

## Verdict

The TCN is a reasonable supervised baseline, and the experiment trail is unusually careful about group splits, checksums, ablations, and rejecting regressions. The main risk is not that TCN is intrinsically wrong. It is that the internal window test is small and source-concentrated for several classes, the first external creator diagnostic drops sharply, and the six-creator benchmark has no locked independent labels yet. The historical web/worker prototype is outside the active project scope. Internal model quality and unseen-creator generalization remain separate claims.

## Evidence and concerns

1. **The internal score is promising but narrow.** V6-C reports 1,971 test windows, end-to-end macro F1 0.7933 and accuracy 0.8290; V6-F reports macro F1 0.8172 and accuracy 0.8417 on the same keys. V6-F raises unknown recall from 0.7458 to 0.8591, but its MM-Fit w13 accuracy is 8.58 percentage points lower and held-out YouTube present-class F1 is 8.4 points lower than V6-C. It also loses recall on lunge and push-up. Keep both as frozen comparison candidates rather than promoting V6-F on one aggregate number. Evidence: `ml/artifacts/v6c/model/metrics.json`, `ml/artifacts/v6f_multistream_gate/model/`, and their per-group/class reports.
2. **Four classes have very weak internal test support.** Pull-up has 4 windows, lat pulldown 7, deadlift 13, bench press 19. Deadlift in V6-F has 13 true windows and 52 predictions, with only 11 correct. Per-class F1 is therefore unstable and may conceal false positives. Of 1,971 test windows, 1,139 are from four MM-Fit subjects and 682 from one YouTube video (92.4% from five groups). The group-disjoint split is good, but overlapping windows are correlated and cannot stand in for independent people or creators. Evidence: split assignments and test predictions in the V6-C/V6-F artifacts.
3. **Class and recording source are partly confounded.** In the V6-C training split, dumbbell row and sit-up occur only in MM-Fit; lat pulldown and pull-up only in WorkoutFitness-video. Most unknown windows come from YouTube material. This can let the model learn filming/pose-estimator signatures as well as exercise motion. Video/clip grouping for WorkoutFitness-video does not itself establish that people are disjoint; this is an unverified leakage risk, not a demonstrated leak. Evidence: `ml/artifacts/v6c/model/split_assignments.csv`.
4. **External generalization is the largest measured warning.** The three-video G2 creator diagnostic has 1,198 windows and only seven supported exercise labels plus unknown. V6-C macro F1 on the present labels is 0.3039, accuracy 0.4917. BiGRU, transformer, adaptive GCN, and an equal ensemble are in the same low range (roughly 0.27–0.30 macro F1), so changing backbone alone did not solve it. This diagnostic was also used for provisional calibration and has draft annotation cautions: it is not a locked final external test. It is nevertheless strong evidence of domain-shift risk. Evidence: `ml/artifacts/g2/creator_comparison/report/system_comparison.csv`, `coverage_by_creator.csv`, `ml/artifacts/calibration/README.md`.
5. **The new six-creator benchmark is correctly quarantined but unfinished.** The first visual pass found 26 trim candidates, five rejects, two unknown relabels, two variant reviews, and two regions needing splits among 37 draft regions. Four supplemental pose regions make the next review queue 41 rows. No independent timeline/boundary review has locked these labels; the initial candidate coverage overstated the number of clearly supported classes. No defensible final 14-class creator score exists yet. Evidence: `PROJECT_HANDOFF.md` and `ml/artifacts/youtube_creator_benchmark_v1/dense_review_evidence/`.
6. **The internal test has informed many rounds of architecture selection.** Validation-based early stopping and threshold selection are appropriate, but repeatedly choosing among V6 variants after inspecting the same 1,971 test windows makes that test a development set in practice. The 0.8172 macro F1 should be described as an internal development result, not an unbiased final performance claim.
7. **Recognition is still a research pipeline.** The active ML target has 14 exercise classes plus unknown, but it has not yet been packaged as the planned local Python application. Pose preprocessing, temporal inference, threshold/decoder, repetition logic, and calorie estimation still need one reconciled local inference path. Notebook gains do not yet establish complete-video, repetition-count, or calorie accuracy. The MET-based calorie estimates and confidence ranges have not been validated against measured calorie ground truth.
8. **Ablations show good scientific discipline.** The added long-video training data improved oracle exercise F1 while harming the gate and end-to-end F1, so it was rejected. Visibility features and temporal resampling were reported separately; the ST-GCN gate failed; the apparent G2 gain was traced to seven lat-pulldown windows. These are reasons to trust the recorded *diagnostics*, even though they do not prove application readiness.

## Prioritized next work

1. **CPU: finish the six-creator independent annotation audit.** Use existing landmarks and review sheets. Resolve boundaries, variants, background/unknown, reviewer disagreements, and class coverage. Record absent classes as absent rather than filling them with guesses. Keep these creators out of training, calibration, and threshold selection. The current Notebook 29C review queue is a preparation aid, not label lock.
2. **CPU: one frozen external comparison.** Score frozen V6-C and V6-F with previously fixed thresholds and identical preprocessing only after labels are locked. Report per-creator and per-class support, confusion, macro F1 on explicitly present labels, unknown recall/false acceptance, and segment-level F1 at IoU thresholds plus boundary error. Bootstrap by creator/video, not by correlated windows; with six creators, confidence intervals will still be wide. Do not claim all 14 classes unless all 14 have valid independent support.
3. **T4 only after that diagnosis: improve data coverage first.** Build a separate creator-diverse training/development set for weak and source-confounded classes, with several people/views per class and realistic unknown transitions. Audit pose detection and training/inference normalization. Use creator-disjoint validation; reserve a genuinely untouched final set. Retrain the same simple baseline before adding complexity, use more than one seed, and calibrate on development creators only.
4. **CPU implementation, T4 only for subsequent training: build the local inference path.** Implement one local Python pipeline using the exact saved pose schema, normalization, FPS/windowing, model/gate, threshold, decoder, and 14-class taxonomy. Evaluate continuous-video recognition, repetition counting, and calorie error separately; window accuracy cannot substitute for these product metrics.
5. **Defer expensive architecture work.** PoseC3D or RGB/pose comparisons are worthwhile only if a clean external benchmark shows pose noise or pose-only ambiguity remains the bottleneck after data/label fixes. Self-supervised pretraining should follow a clean supervised baseline, not precede it.

## Research comparison

TCNs are established for temporal action segmentation, so using one here is defensible. Strong action-segmentation evaluations report frame accuracy together with segmental edit and F1 at overlap thresholds because frame/window accuracy misses over-segmentation and boundary errors ([MS-TCN, CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Abu_Farha_MS-TCN_Multi-Stage_Temporal_Convolutional_Network_for_Action_Segmentation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf)). Larger action-recognition benchmarks explicitly test unseen subjects and camera views ([NTU RGB+D](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.02808)); MM-Fit itself addresses recognition and repetition logging ([MM-Fit](https://mmfit.github.io/)). PoseConv3D reports better robustness to pose-estimation noise and cross-dataset transfer than some graph methods ([CVPR 2022](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Duan_Revisiting_Skeleton-Based_Action_Recognition_CVPR_2022_paper.html)); that supports a future controlled comparison, not an assumption that it will fix this dataset. Published accuracy numbers on different labels, sensors, and splits are not directly comparable to this project's scores.

**Decision rule:** Freeze the current research candidates, lock the external labels, then let creator-level errors determine whether the next investment is data/pose quality, gate calibration, temporal segmentation, or architecture. Do not spend T4 time on a bigger model while the external benchmark and product path remain unresolved.
